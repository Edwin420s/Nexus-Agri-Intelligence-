# Nexus Product Analysis

## Problem-solution fit hypothesis
The proposed gap is not lack of general information alone; it is the difficulty of evaluating whether mixed evidence is current, local, comparable, complete, and sufficient for a real decision. Nexus's hypothesis is that a traceable dossier reduces research fragmentation and helps users identify what to verify next.

## Value proposition
For Kenyan agribusiness entrepreneurs evaluating an opportunity, Nexus organizes accessible evidence and exposes assumptions and gaps, unlike an unstructured answer that may hide missing inputs. The product's value depends on data access and quality; without relevant data, it must transparently return an insufficient result.

## Why tomato farming in Kirinyaga
This is a bounded demonstration scenario, not a claim that tomatoes are necessarily the best crop or that Kirinyaga is validated as the most attractive market. It offers a concrete way to test market, cost, water, seasonal, logistics, and buyer-access evidence requirements. User research and real data must validate the scenario.

## Competitive alternatives
- Manual web research and spreadsheets: flexible but time-consuming and often difficult to audit.
- Farmer networks and agronomists: valuable local expertise, but access and coverage vary.
- Agricultural market information platforms: may provide prices or advisories, but may not combine a user's assumptions, evidence conflicts, and a decision dossier.
- General-purpose AI: broad reasoning but may not provide a controlled evidence workflow by default.
- Nexus: proposes to connect evidence retrieval, quality checks, explicit gaps, deterministic policy, and a traceable report.

This comparison is qualitative product reasoning, not an independently researched competitive study.

## Main assumptions to validate
1. Users have a recurring, high-value need for structured opportunity assessment.
2. Accessible data sources contain enough local, dated, comparable records.
3. Users trust a transparent dossier more than an unstructured response.
4. The time saved exceeds the cost and effort of gathering source data.
5. The user can act on the next steps produced by Nexus.
6. Users or institutions are willing to pay for the service, or a viable sponsor/partner model exists.

## Failure modes
- Correct-looking dossier from unsuitable data.
- Old prices presented as current.
- National statistics generalized to a particular farm.
- One source treated as independent corroboration.
- Cost model omits post-harvest, financing, land, or market costs.
- Model prose makes a stronger claim than the cited evidence.
- A `SUPPORTED` evidence status is mistaken for “this business will succeed.”
- User sees synthetic fixtures as real data.

## Strategic recommendation
Prioritize data quality and evidence lineage over adding more agent roles. The next engineering gate is a verified source query and a real model call. The next product-validation gate is a small set of interviews and a task-based test with intended users. Avoid claiming adoption, impact, or predictive accuracy until measured.
